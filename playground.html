How Zip Works

  • Rule 1: Start at cell 1 and draw a single continuous line connecting the numbered dots in ascending order (1 → 2 → 3...).
  • Rule 2: The path must visit every single cell on the grid exactly once (Hamiltonian path).
  • Rule 3: Moves are strictly orthogonal (up, down, left, right). No diagonals allowed.
  • Rule 4: Thick red borders are walls. Your path cannot cross through walls.
